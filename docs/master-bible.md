# Moolank99 Master Bible

## Product purpose

Moolank99 is a numerology-based self-awareness and personal-growth product.

Numerology is the reflective starting blueprint. It is not a medical diagnosis, scientific measurement, or guaranteed prediction system.

## Three-blueprint model

### Birth Blueprint

Derived from deterministic calculations such as Moolank, Bhagyank, Namank, and compound numbers. It describes possible tendencies and symbolic themes.

### Living Blueprint

Derived from the user's present context: goals, habits, mood, energy, stress, focus, relationships, recent experiences, and self-reported challenges.

### Desired Blueprint

The qualities and outcomes the user intentionally wants to develop.

The product helps the user move from current reality toward chosen growth without treating the Birth Blueprint as fixed destiny.

## Five core engines

1. **Numerology Calculation Engine** — deterministic calculations only.
2. **Knowledge Engine** — versioned, reviewed numerology and growth content.
3. **Human State Engine** — current self-reported state and trends.
4. **Guidance Engine** — selects one relevant priority, action, and reflection.
5. **Progress Engine** — tracks practices, check-ins, feedback, and change over time.

## Knowledge rules

Every knowledge statement must identify its source class:

- `traditional_wisdom`
- `reflective_framework`
- `behavioural_practice`
- `user_reported_experience`
- `mixed`

Traditional numerology content must use language such as **may**, **can**, **often**, **possible tendency**, and **possible expression**. It must not be written as a guaranteed identity claim.

## AI boundaries

AI may:

- retrieve approved knowledge,
- combine Birth Blueprint with user goals and current state,
- simplify language,
- remove repetition,
- generate structured reflections and summaries.

AI may not:

- invent new numerology rules,
- diagnose mental or physical conditions,
- present numerology as scientific proof,
- silently convert user feedback into universal truth,
- make deterministic claims about fate.

## Standard report dimensions

1. Personality
2. Relationships
3. Career
4. Money
5. Growth
6. Wellbeing
7. Purpose
8. Potential

Each dimension should contain:

- summary,
- core pattern,
- healthy expression,
- stress expression,
- strengths,
- growth edges,
- life examples,
- actionable guidance,
- reflection questions,
- recommended practices,
- progress indicators.

## MVP knowledge scope

The first knowledge release will include:

- Moolank 1–9,
- reusable node structure,
- eight report dimensions,
- reflection questions,
- low-risk behavioural practices,
- source and evidence labels,
- version history.

Bhagyank, Namank, compound numbers, chakras, remedies, relationship patterns, and advanced transformation plans follow only after the Moolank structure is validated.

## Content workflow

1. Draft structured node.
2. Review symbolic claims and language.
3. Separate traditional wisdom from behavioural guidance.
4. Validate JSON/schema structure.
5. Product review for usefulness and safety.
6. Publish with version and review status.
7. Collect resonance feedback without treating it as proof.

## Product output rule

A daily guidance response should normally contain:

- one current priority,
- one short explanation,
- one practical action,
- one reflection question,
- one review point.

The product should prefer clarity and consistency over overwhelming users with many recommendations.
