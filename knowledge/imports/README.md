# Recovered Imports

Place old conversation exports, roadmaps, screenshots transcriptions, and legacy research files here before converting them into canonical knowledge.

## Rules

- Imported files are evidence/recovery material, not app-facing truth.
- Preserve the original text as much as possible.
- Record where and when the import came from.
- Do not load files from this folder into the app.
- Review recovered content against current evidence and safety standards before canonicalization.

## Naming examples

```text
conversation-export-phase-6.15.md
moolank-1-master-roadmap-recovered.md
anger-canonical-v1.2-recovered.md
```

After review, move accepted knowledge into `evidence/` and `canonical/`, then update the index, status, changelog, loader, tests, and deployment.
